Questions people ask

Before you give it

Will they know it is from me?

Yes. The email says "there's a letter for you" with your name on it, the envelope has their name and "with love, from you", and your letter is the first thing they see when it opens.

What if they are not good with email?

Print the card we send you and put it under the tree. It has a short address and a code to point a phone camera at, so anyone in the room can open it with them. The book is emailed to them as well, so nothing is lost.

Can I give one to Mom and one to Dad?

Yes. Each gift is its own sealed envelope, with its own letter, voice note and day, so write each letter for the person opening it. The first is $49 and each one after that is $39, and they each get their own The Estate Organizer to fill in.

Can I change the letter or the day after I pay?

Yes, any time before it sends, from the link in your email. Once it has gone, it has gone, so we hold it until the minute you chose.

What do they need to use the book?

A computer, laptop or tablet and a free PDF reader, which every one of them already has. They type into the boxes, save, and print whatever pages they want. No account, no app, no subscription.

Is it a will?

No. The will and powers of attorney are legal documents done with an attorney. The Estate Organizer is everything else: where things are, who to call, and what they want. It has a page to note where the legal documents are kept.

Five minutes now. A sealed envelope on Christmas morning, your voice inside, and a book their family will be glad they had.
