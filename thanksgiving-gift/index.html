Questions people ask

Before you give it

Will they know it is from me?

Yes. The email comes from you, with your name in the subject line. When they open it, your message is the first thing they read.

What if they are not good with email?

Print the card from your email at home and set it at their place at the table. It has a short address and a code to point a phone camera at, so anyone in the room can open it with them. The book is emailed to them as well, so nothing is lost.

Is there still time before Thanksgiving?

Yes. Set it up any time before Thanksgiving morning and it arrives at 9am on Thursday, November 26, their time. If you are later than that, pick any day and time and it arrives then.

Can I give one to Mom and one to Dad?

Yes. Each gift is its own email, with its own message, voice note and day, so write each message for the person opening it. Once your first gift is set up, the email we send you has a link to give more, at a lower price for each extra one. Each person gets their own Estate Organizer to fill in.

Can I change my message or the day after I pay?

Yes, any time before it sends, from the link in your email. Once it has gone, it has gone, so we hold it until the minute you chose.

What do they need to use the book?

A computer, laptop or tablet and a free PDF reader, which every one of them already has. They type into the boxes, save, and print whatever pages they want. No account, no app, no subscription.

Is it a will?

No. The will and powers of attorney are legal documents done with an attorney. The Estate Organizer is everything else: where things are, who to call, and what they want. It has a page to note where the legal documents are kept.

Give them the one thing nobody else will

Five minutes now. An email from you on Thanksgiving morning, with your voice in it, and a book their family will be glad they had.
