## Cut for lack of a source

**Social Security, the largest hole in the pack.** Every ssa.gov URL returned an
Akamai 403 to both the fetch tool and a browser-user-agent curl when the pack was
built, so none of the following appears anywhere in the post:

- The $255 lump-sum death payment figure. Not stated, not hinted at, not hedged.
- The two-year deadline for claiming that payment.
- SSA's own wording on survivor benefit eligibility, who qualifies, and at what age.
- Any survivor benefit amount or percentage.
- The SSA-8 form and any document checklist SSA publishes for a survivor claim.

What the post does say about Social Security comes only from usa.gov and
medicare.gov: that the funeral director should report the death and SSA will notify
Medicare, the 1-800-772-1213 and TTY numbers, and the rule that the SSA cannot pay
benefits for the month of a recipient's death. Everything else points the reader to
ssa.gov and the phone line, with no figures attached, and the post says out loud
that it is withholding numbers rather than pretending the topic is covered.

**Also cut:**

- **Passport cancellation detail.** travel.state.gov was Cloudflare-blocked, so the
  CLASP mailing address and the "not legally required to cancel" line are absent.
  The post says only what usa.gov says: you can return the passport to the State
  Department for cancellation.
- **A number of certified death certificate copies.** No government page gives a
  figure for a domestic death. The 20-free-copies figure applies to a Consular
  Report of Death Abroad and is not used. The post says there is no national answer.
- **USPS mail forwarding duration.** The 12-month figure is on third-party sites
  only, not in the fetched USPS text. No duration is stated.
- **NAIC locator turnaround time.** The "up to 90 business days" figure sat behind
  a CAPTCHA wall on the tool page. No processing time is given.
- **Any generalization of the California DMV process.** Only one state was verified
  for driver's license and vehicle handling, so the DMV 22 form and the PNO status
  are labeled California only.
- **The phrase "basic exclusion amount"** for the federal estate tax threshold. The
  IRS page shows a filing threshold table but does not use that phrase in the
  fetched content, so the post says filing threshold instead.
- **State estate and inheritance taxes.** Mentioned as existing in some states,
  with no state named and no figure, because no state page was verified for this.
- **Any national probate rule, timeline or small-estate threshold.** None exists.
  Only the two named state examples are given, and both are labeled as such.
