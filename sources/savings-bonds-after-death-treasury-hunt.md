## Cut or not verified

- Any newer total of matured unredeemed bonds than the April 30, 2021 figure. The report read today is the only official figure found; the post states its date.
- Any replacement search tool. TreasuryDirect names none; it points to the forms page, so the post points there too.
- The 'never expire' wording. Treasury's report says paper bonds 'never expire'; the post paraphrases this as 'do not expire' to keep absolutes out of the copy.
- Distribution detail (FS Form 1522, FS Form 4000, FS Form 5396, electronic bonds needing a TreasuryDirect account). Verified on the non-administered estates page, cut for length.
- TreasuryDirect asks for 'a certified copy of the death certificate' and also says to 'send only copies of documents like the death certificate'. Both lines are reported as the agency states them.
- Tax treatment of bond interest. Not stated; the post repeats TreasuryDirect's pointer to a tax advisor and IRS Publications 550, 17 and 559.
