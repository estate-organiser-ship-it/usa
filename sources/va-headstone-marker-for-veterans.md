## Cut or not verified

- The fax number. It renders through a script on va.gov and is blank in the fetched text, so the post says 'by fax' without a number.
- Processing times for delivery. The VA page gives only the 30 and 60 day status-check points, which are used.
- Memorial headstones for remains not recovered, buried at sea, donated or scattered. Verified on cem.va.gov/hmm, cut for length.
- Burial allowance amounts. Not read today, so only the form page's description of the allowance is used, with no figure.
